The pit is not the carton.

Fresh produce is belts, not a pit. Almost no carton has a futures contract. Listed names are financial weather around the carton — growers who report, fertilizer and iron, trucks and boats, cold rooms, and the buyers who set programs. They are not the AMS shipping-point tape and they are not your invoice.

Delayed public quotes, not advice, not a live feed, not a cleared produce trade. OJ=F is frozen concentrated orange juice. DBA is grains and livestock. Iceberg, berries, and greenhouse tomatoes still do not have a ticker.

These are the relationships, not a trading signal. The carton still clears on AMS FOB and your PO.

Fertilizer (NTR, MOS, CF) rips

Next planting's cost floor moves. Farm-gate and then FOB lag — this week's lettuce does not reprice overnight.

Watch NTR · MOS · CF

Truck (KNX) rips on tightness

Landed GTA / DC can rise with origin FOB flat. Salinas and Yakima feel it first.

Watch KNX

Ocean (ZIM) spikes

Chile grapes, Peru asparagus / avocado / blueberry, NZ kiwi get more expensive to land. Counter-season is a boat.

Watch ZIM

Grocery (KR, WMT, COST) dumps

Programs get leaned on even in a short crop. Retail is bargaining power, not a farm print.

Watch KR · WMT · COST

Foodservice (SYY, USFD) rips

Restaurant pull for kits, tomatoes, fries. A bag of salad is not NASS head lettuce.

Watch SYY · USFD · PFGC

Peso (USDMXN) weakens

Mexico winter veg and Hass can print cheaper in USD at Nogales / McAllen. Ontario greenhouse sells spec against that floor.

Watch USDMXN=X · VFF · AVO

USD strengthens vs CAD

The same USDA / AMS dollar FOB costs more in CAD. Compass farm prints move without the field moving.

Watch USDCAD=X

OJ futures rip

Florida freeze or Brazil pulp. Fresh navels on a retail rack are a different desk. Pair with Alico, not a carton of Valencias.

Watch OJ=F · ALCO

Cold storage (COLD) rips

Rooms are tight or rates are up — apples, pears, onions, kiwi, fries. Storage crops, not berries.

Watch COLD
