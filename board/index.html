How the majors actually bench

Big produce companies do not bid off NASS. NASS is a month-end national average for USDA indexes. The trade tape is USDA AMS shipping-point FOB that morning, then a program on top of it.

  1. Step 1

    Farm / grove

    Grower settlement, not public. Closest public floor is NASS prices received (monthly, all grades).

  2. Step 2

    Packed FOB / shipping point

    USDA AMS Market News, daily, by district, pack, size, grade. This is the actual industry benchmark.

  3. Step 3

    Crossing / import FOB

    AMS at Nogales, McAllen, Philadelphia docks. Census unit values are too late for a PO.

  4. Step 4

    Landed DC

    FOB + freight + duty + yield. Sysco and a retailer live here.

  5. Step 5

    Program / kit / brand

    Contract: AMS FOB ± a schedule, or a fixed kit price. The SKU is no longer the commodity.

A Salinas / Yakima grower-shipper

Sells packed cartons

AMS shipping-point FOB for that district that morning, plus pack-out. NASS is what USDA prints at month-end. They do not bid off it.

Farm-to-FOB is pack, cool, carton, and their margin — often 15–40% over farm-gate on fresh.

Driscoll's

Berries, genetics + logistics

Clamshell FOB by district week. Open-market AMS berry FOB is the tape; their genetics usually clear a 15–20% premium over generic. NASS strawberry cwt is too coarse for a clamshell.

The brand is the spread. Independent berry FOB is not the Driscoll invoice.

Taylor Farms / Fresh Express

Fresh-cut salad kits

They buy Salinas and Yuma head/romaine FOB as an input. The customer (Sysco, a grocer) pays for film, labor, yield, and a slot. Kit price is not lettuce cwt.

A bag can sit 3–6× the farm print after shrink. NASS head lettuce is their raw, not their SKU.

Dole (fruit, post veg sale)

Bananas, pineapple, tropcial programs

Global Cavendish / MD2 contract curves, not a US NASS series. US leafy greens were the volatile book they stepped back from.

The floor is the boat and the ripening room, not Agricultural Prices.

Grimmway / Bolthouse

Carrots (cello + juice)

Year-round California cello programs. NASS carrot cwt is the farm floor; they sell a branded bag and a juice line.

Two firms, most of US fresh carrots. A jumbo/juice quote is a different plant.

Mission Produce

Avocados, origin-switch + ripen

Mexico / Peru / California crossing FOB plus a ripening spec. Avocados are not a useful NASS monthly series. They move fruit between hemispheres because they own rooms, not because of a dashboard.

Ripe-and-ready is the product. Green FOB is not the invoice.

Sysco / US Foods

Broadline foodservice

Landed cost + yield + house margin. They buy Taylor / Dole / house programs. The PO is not an AMS print.

If you sell to them you are on their spec and their clock, not on NASS.

Ontario greenhouse (tomatoes, cukes, peppers)

Local high-spec, winter vs Mexico

Their FOB vs Nogales / McAllen FOB + freight + CFIA. AAFC wholesale is the destination ask, not their settlement.

Winter Mexico sets the floor. Ontario sells spec, not the cheapest landed round.
